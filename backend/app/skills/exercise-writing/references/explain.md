# explain: say the rule in your own words

Fields: `question`, `key_points` and `explanation`.

- The question asks the learner to explain one part of the rule in their own words
  (when to use it, how it differs from a confusable form), answerable in one or two
  sentences without grammar terms.
- `key_points` lists the 1-4 points a good answer covers, as short phrases.
- `explanation` is a model answer in plain words.
