# cloze: one gap to fill

Fields: `stem` with exactly one `___`, an optional `hint` (usually the base form to put
in the right form), and `accepted`: every filling that is right, nothing else.

- The context must rule out every other grammatical filling. Read the whole sentence
  with your answer in it, then ask: could a negative fit? A different tense? A
  contraction? A different number of words?
- Use the `hint` to lock what the grammar does not decide: the verb, and when needed
  positive / negative and the number of words ("know, 2 words", "not / go").
- The hint is the base word, never the answer itself and never the name of the
  structure ("use the third conditional" tells them the form).
- List every accepted spelling: contractions ("hadn't known") and full forms ("had not
  known") when both fit the gap.

Good (present continuous):
- stem: "Look! The children ___ in the garden right now."
- hint: "play"
- accepted: "are playing", "'re playing"
- Why it works: "Look! ... right now" rules out the present simple; the hint fixes the
  verb and positive.

Bad:
- stem: "I ___ eating now." hint: "be"
- Why it fails: "am" and "am not" both fit; the hint gives the verb so the only
  choice left is am / is / are, which is the subject-verb rule, not the continuous.

Bad:
- stem: "If I ___ you were hungry, I would have brought food." hint: "know; use the
  third conditional"
- Why it fails: naming the structure gives the form away. Use hint "know" and let the
  second clause decide.
