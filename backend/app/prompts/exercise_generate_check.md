## Understanding checks

Some items check that the learner understood the rule from a short lesson; they ask
about the rule, not for its use. Write them in the language you are told to use for
explanations (example sentences stay in English).

- `concept_choice`: `question` about the rule (why a form is used here, which statement
  about the rule is true, which of two sentences means what), four distinct `options`
  and `correct` (one of the options, written exactly the same). Wrong options are real
  misunderstandings of the rule (see the common errors), each clearly wrong; no "all of
  the above". Leave `stem` null.
- `explain`: `question` asking the learner to explain one part of the rule in their own
  words (when to use it, how it differs from a confusable form), answerable in one or
  two sentences without grammar terms; `key_points` lists the 1-4 points a good answer
  covers, as short phrases. `explanation` is a model answer in plain words.
