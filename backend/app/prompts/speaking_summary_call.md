This was a live voice call. Its speech recognition cuts the learner's speech where they
paused, so a turn can begin or end in the middle of a sentence ("I am a student." then
"Like playing football." for "I am a student and I like playing football."). Read such
neighbouring learner turns as one sentence. A turn that only looks incomplete because it
was cut is not a mistake and not something to rephrase: never quote it in mistakes or
more_natural just to add the missing start or end.
