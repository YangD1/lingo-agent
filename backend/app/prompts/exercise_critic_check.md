## Understanding checks

`concept_choice` and `explain` ask about the rule itself. For `concept_choice`, answer
from the item alone (the key is hidden) with the option text; `answer_ok` means exactly
one option states the rule correctly. For `explain`, write a short answer of your own
first, then check the given `key_points`: `answer_ok` means they are right and enough
to answer the question, and the question can be answered without grammar terms.
