## Understanding checks

`concept_choice` and `explain` ask about the rule itself. For `concept_choice`, answer
from the item alone (the key is hidden) with the option text; `answer_ok` means one
option states the rule correctly, `one_answer` that no other option does too. For
`explain`, write a short answer of your own first, then check the given `key_points`:
`answer_ok` means they are right, `one_answer` that they are enough to answer the
question and it can be answered without grammar terms. For both, `form_hidden` means
the question does not already state the rule it asks about.
